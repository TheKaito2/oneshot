# OneShot live demo: shot list

Target: about 4:30 in total.
- **0:00 to 0:11:** the intro: the hype showreel (`Krist/video-editing/oneshot-intro/oneshot-intro-hype.mp4`).
- **0:11 to 4:30:** the live demo below.

All English. Two devices:
- **iPad:** the counter. Scan and charge here.
- **iPhone:** the owner's phone. Cart, sync, reports and settings here.

Each beat gives the **device**, **where to go**, **what to tap**, and **what to say**. Times are cumulative.
Lines in *italics* are voice-over; you can record it live or on top afterwards.

---

## Before you press record (prep, not filmed)

1. **Both devices:**
   - Fully charged.
   - Do Not Disturb on.
   - Clock showing nothing odd.
   - Wi-Fi on.
2. **Signed in:** both devices are signed in to the **same account** (Settings › Account), so sync can be shown.
3. **iPad Settings:**
   - Demo & sample data › **Demo camera OFF** (the real camera is filmed).
   - Refund PIN off.
4. **Product data:**
   - The iPad already knows the 12 snacks.
   - Reports needs history: on a fresh install, Settings › Demo & sample data › **Add two weeks of sample sales**. This only works while the books are empty.
5. **On the counter:** 6 to 8 known snacks spread out flat. Keep **one product OneShot doesn't know** aside, for Teach (any pack, e.g. a drink box).
6. **Second camera:** if you have one, put it on a tripod over the counter for a wide shot of the iPad above the snacks. Otherwise film the counter with the iPhone first, then switch it to screen recording.
7. **Screen recording:** turn it on on each device (Control Center › Screen Recording) at the start of its beats. You'll cut the clips together afterwards.

## Beat 1: The problem (0:11 to 0:35)

**Wide shot of the counter, iPad on its stand.**

*"A small Thai shop at rush hour. The cashier scans each snack one by one, or adds it up in their head. A long
queue, and mistakes."*

## Beat 2: One photo, whole counter (0:35 to 1:20) — the hero beat

**iPad, landscape. Tap Checkout** (sidebar or top tabs).
1. **The live boxes:** hold the iPad over the snacks. Live boxes with names appear.
   *"OneShot sees every pack on the counter, live, on the device."*
2. **Scan:** tap **Scan** (the big coral button).
   - The shutter flashes.
   - Green boxes appear, with a chime.
   - The cart fills on the right, with the total.
   *"One tap. Every item named and priced, in about a second."*
3. **A yellow item (optional):** if a box turns **yellow**, tap the yellow item in the cart and pick the right product.
   *"If it isn't sure, it never guesses a price. It asks. And that pick teaches it for next time."*

## Beat 3: Get paid (1:20 to 1:45)

**iPad.**
1. **Show the QR:** tap **Charge ฿…**. The PromptPay QR appears with the amount filled in, and the total is
   spoken in Thai.
2. **Turn it round:** tap **Turn to customer** to show the QR the right way up for the customer.
   *"PromptPay QR with the amount already in it, read out in Thai. Or cash, with the change worked out."*
3. **Take the payment:** tap **Paid**, then **New sale**.

## Beat 4: Teach a new product in 10 seconds (1:45 to 2:25)

**iPad, Checkout. Tap Teach** (the viewfinder-plus icon, bottom right).
1. **Photos:** put the unknown product on its own. Tap **Take photo** 8 times, turning the pack a little each time.
2. **Details:** type the name in English (and Thai), price **฿15**, then tap **Save item**.
3. **Scan it:** put it back with the others and tap **Scan**. It's recognised.
   *"New product? Eight photos and a price. No retraining, no cloud. It's recognised right away."*

## Beat 5: The owner's phone (2:25 to 3:05)

**iPhone, portrait. Tap the coral Scan circle** (centre of the bottom bar).
1. **Scan:** point it at 3 or 4 snacks and tap **Scan**. The cart slides in from the right.
2. **Check against the photo:** swipe between **Scanner** and **Cart** (or use the switch at the top).
   *"On iPhone, swipe between the photo and the cart to double-check every item."*
3. **Add item:** tap **+** in the cart header. Show the three options: **Scan with OneShot**, **Scan a barcode**, **Choose from the list**. Pick a barcode on a pack.
   *"Barcodes still work when you need them."*

## Beat 6: Sync across devices (3:05 to 3:25)

**iPhone. Tap Home.** The iPad's sales from beats 2 and 4 are in the totals and in Activity.
Sync runs every few minutes. If they aren't there yet, open Settings › Account › **Sync now** off camera, then come back to Home.
*"Sign in with Apple or Google, and the iPad and iPhone stay in step. It's optional; everything works offline."*

## Beat 7: Reports (3:25 to 4:00)

**iPhone. Tap Reports.**
1. **Period:** tap the **Today ⌄** pill and choose **This month**.
2. **The trend:** tap **⤢** on *Net sales by day*, then drag a finger across the chart to read single days.
3. **Top products:** close it, scroll, and open **Top products**: full names, by money or by units.
4. **Bank statement:** scroll to **Bank statement** and tap the preview icon.
   *"Daily totals, best sellers, what sells together. And a monthly statement a bank can check, for a small-business loan."*

## Beat 8: Privacy and close (4:00 to 4:30)

**iPhone. Tap Settings.**
1. **Show** *Help improve OneShot* (off) and *Privacy policy*. Don't change anything.
   *"Recognition runs on the device. Nothing leaves it unless the owner opts in, and faces are blurred if they do."*
2. **Wide shot of the counter,** with one last **Scan** on the iPad.
   *"OneShot. One photo. The whole counter."*
3. **End card:** the hype showreel's last 2 s (the ONESHOT lockup).

## Filming tips

- **Hands:** keep them out of the frame when you tap Scan. The photo is the star.
- **Light:** even and bright, with no glare on shiny packs. Near a window works well.
- **Pace:** hold every finished state (green boxes, QR, chart) for **2 seconds** before the next tap, so the edit has room.
- **Second take:** record each beat twice. The editor (me) picks the cleaner one.
